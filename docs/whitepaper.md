# Sheath Whitepaper

## 1. Vision and mission

Token launches reward speed and splitting. Whoever arrives first, or spreads funds over the most wallets, takes the largest share at the lowest price. Sheath exists to make the launch itself even-handed: a slice of a given size costs the same for everybody, and one human cannot pose as many.

Mission: give every participant on Solana the same price per slice, the same ceiling, and a guarded path from commitment to token.

## 2. Principles

- **Equal price.** A slice costs the same whenever it is taken. Arriving earlier earns no discount, and two slices cost exactly twice one.
- **One human, one slot.** A uniqueness check from an identity provider limits each verified human to a single capped slot per token. Provider and ceiling are pending.
- **Identity stays private.** Verification runs in the provider's own frame. Sheath receives a verdict, never a face, surname or government document.
- **Guard first.** Commitments rest off-ledger while a round gathers. The token only comes into existence once the round completes.

## 4. Roadmap

1. **Phase 1: Foundation.** Brand, public site, live market board, and the register that keeps every external fact in one place.
2. **Phase 2: Rounds.** Off-ledger commitment flow, identity verification, equal-price accounting.
3. **Phase 3: Formation.** Token creation and delivery of allocations to qualified participants.
4. **Phase 4: Open ecosystem.** Published terms for fees and timing, public repository, partner integrations.
